## What this policy covers

This policy explains how Little Junkers communicates with customers by text message (SMS/MMS), phone, email, and our Randy chat assistant. It also explains how you give consent, opt out, and manage your preferences.

## Types of messages you may receive

**A) Rental and account messages (transactional)**

- Booking confirmations
- Delivery and pickup scheduling and updates
- On-the-way and arrival notices
- Pickup reminders (we text the day before your scheduled pickup)
- Invoices, receipts, and payment links
- Replies to your questions and customer support

**B) Marketing messages (only if you opt in)**

- Occasional offers and announcements from Little Junkers

Some messages are sent automatically. We do **not** send spam.

## How you opt in (consent)

You may agree to receive text messages from Little Junkers in any of these ways:

- At checkout, by leaving **"Keep me updated about this rental"** checked (rental messages) and, if you want offers, checking **"Send me occasional Little Junkers offers"** (marketing messages)
- By giving Randy, our chat assistant, your mobile number and asking for a booking link or a follow-up
- By texting us first at (470) 712-5079
- By asking for texts during a phone call or in writing

**Consent to marketing messages is not a condition of purchase.** You can rent a dumpster without opting in to marketing.

## Message frequency

Rental messages vary with your booking (usually a few per rental, more on delivery and pickup days). Marketing messages, if you opt in, are occasional.

## Message and data rates

**Message and data rates may apply** depending on your mobile plan and carrier.

## How to opt out of texts

Reply **STOP** to any message to stop texts. You will receive one final message confirming your opt-out. If you opt out, we may still contact you by phone or email about an active rental. Reply **START** to receive texts again.

## Help

Reply **HELP** for help, or contact us at **(470) 548-4733** or **info@littlejunkersllc.com**.

## Phone number changes

If you change your phone number, please tell us so we can update your contact information and preferences.

## Privacy

We use your phone number and messaging preferences to:

- Send the rental updates and support you asked for
- Send marketing messages, only if you opted in
- Keep records of consent and opt-out requests, as carriers and the law require

**No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.** Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties, except the messaging providers (Twilio) that deliver our texts on our behalf.

For more details, see our **[Privacy Policy](/privacy)**.
