If you're renting a dumpster for a Peachtree City cleanout, you may be wondering where it can go, whether the truck will fit, and what your HOA allows. Those are worth checking before you choose a delivery date.

## 1. Will a dumpster damage my driveway?

It's the number one fear for local homeowners, and for good reason. Traditional commercial waste companies use large, heavy, industrial rigs. When those trucks squeeze onto a residential driveway, they can crack concrete or tear up landscaping.

### The Little Junkers Difference

We designed our service to avoid this. Instead of a commercial rig, we use a neighborhood-friendly **Ford Super Duty roll-off system**.

- **Check the access:** Our truck is smaller than many commercial roll-off trucks, but driveway condition, slopes and overhead clearance still matter. We'll help you choose a suitable placement.
- **Footprint:** Our bins are about 12 feet long by 8 feet wide. Whether your car can park alongside one depends on the driveway and where the truck can set it down.

## 2. Do I Need a Permit in Peachtree City or Fayetteville?

Don't assume permit rules are the same for every address. A private driveway is usually simpler, but public-street and right-of-way placement can require approval. Check with Peachtree City before scheduling a container on public property or near a cart path.

### Don't Forget the HOA

The city might not care about a dumpster in your driveway, but your Homeowners Association might. Fayette County HOAs can be strict about appearances.

- **The golden rule:** Send a courtesy email to your HOA board with the dates of your project.
- **Plan the location:** Check how long your HOA allows a dumpster to remain and whether it has placement or visibility rules.

## 3. Can't I Just Haul It to the Fayette County Transfer Station Myself?

If you have a pickup truck, a couple of free Saturdays, and a high tolerance for waiting in line at the dump, you *could* DIY it. But once you add up fuel, tipping fees, loading and unloading the truck several times, and the risk of scratching your own vehicle, it adds up fast.

With a roll-off rental, you load once at your own pace, we haul it away, and you get your weekend back.
