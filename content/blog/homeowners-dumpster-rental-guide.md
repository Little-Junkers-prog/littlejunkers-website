So, you're finally tackling that garage cleanout, ripping up old carpet, or remodeling the kitchen. You know you need a dumpster, but if you love your home (and your driveway), a few nagging questions are probably keeping you from booking.

*Will a massive truck crack my concrete? Will my HOA fine me? Do I need a permit from the city?*

As a local team here in Fayette County, we hear these concerns every day from our neighbors. Let's go through them.

## 1. Honestly... Is a Dumpster Going to Ruin My Driveway?

It's the number one fear for local homeowners, and for good reason. Traditional commercial waste companies use large, heavy, industrial rigs. When those trucks squeeze onto a residential driveway, they can crack concrete or tear up landscaping.

### The Little Junkers Difference

We designed our service to avoid this. Instead of a commercial rig, we use a neighborhood-friendly **Ford Super Duty roll-off system**.

- **Driveway-safe design:** Our truck is lighter and more maneuverable. We can back up your driveway and set the bin where you need it.
- **Compact footprint:** Our pink bins fit in a standard driveway space, leaving room to park your car (or your golf cart) next to them.

## 2. Do I Need a Permit in Peachtree City or Fayetteville?

The short answer: **not if the dumpster is on your private property.** If the bin fits in your driveway, you do *not* need a city or county permit. If your driveway is too steep or short and the bin has to go on a public street, Peachtree City requires a right-of-way encroachment permit.

### Don't Forget the HOA

The city might not care about a dumpster in your driveway, but your Homeowners Association might. Fayette County HOAs can be strict about appearances.

- **The golden rule:** Send a courtesy email to your HOA board with the dates of your project.
- **The pink bin advantage:** Nobody wants to look at a rusty, dented dumpster for a week. Our clean, bright pink bins show your neighbors you care about how the neighborhood looks, even during a cleanout.

## 3. Can't I Just Haul It to the Fayette County Transfer Station Myself?

If you have a pickup truck, a couple of free Saturdays, and a high tolerance for waiting in line at the dump, you *could* DIY it. But once you add up fuel, tipping fees, loading and unloading the truck several times, and the risk of scratching your own vehicle, it adds up fast.

With a roll-off rental, you load once at your own pace, we haul it away, and you get your weekend back.
