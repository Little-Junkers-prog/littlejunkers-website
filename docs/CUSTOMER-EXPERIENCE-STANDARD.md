# Customer Experience and Conversion Standard — November 28, 2026

**Direction:** Owner endorsed the November 28 conversion-design recommendations and requested charter updates, page revisions and checkout design V1. Implementation on a review branch only. All proposed screen/copy variations require review; financial and operational rules are not approved by a design endorsement.

**Governing:** PROJECT-CHARTER §12; STRATEGY-ARCHIVE §30; ADR-CX-001–004. This standard adds a measurable customer-experience gate; it does not replace the existing Brand Guide, approved page templates, SEO migration, consent/security rules or unified-checkout-v1 architecture.

## North star

Make Little Junkers the easiest local dumpster rental company to confidently do business with. Design a guided buying experience, not merely a better booking form. Five non-negotiable principles:

1. **Clarity before commitment.** Reveal canonical starting prices and location-specific delivery charges without collecting contact details. State what is known, unknown and included. Price/availability must be verified before payment.
2. **Confidence before conversion.** Use optional project help, actual equipment photos, confirmed dimensions, approximate volume examples and an explanation of included disposal weight. Explain why a size is suggested; let the customer choose a smaller/larger size or ask for help. Experienced/returning buyers can skip the recommendation.
3. **Transparency before persuasion.** Itemize all mandatory charges before payment, explain conditional charges and cancellation/change policies when approved, use dated sourced comparisons even when a competitor is less expensive, no false scarcity or unsupported guarantees.
4. **Convenience before complexity.** One in-site checkout, preserve context, no mandatory account, one customer-details entry, relevant fields only, clear edit/back actions, visible mobile continuation and price summary, accessible call/text assistance.
5. **Proof before promises.** Use real local photos and owners, authentic sourced reviews (no invented ratings, quotes or counts), real operational availability and honest failure/unavailable states. Never promise property protection against all damage, instant delivery or a guaranteed time not supported by operations.

## Marketing page acceptance criteria

- One clear customer job per route; first meaningful content answers the visitor's intent. One primary commercial action per viewport, with descriptive links/secondary research tools. The pre-booking CTA says Check prices, See my estimate or Choose your dumpster; use See prices & availability only if real availability can be checked. Do not present payment or booking as available on a read-only preview.
- Home: photo-led value proposition, city/ZIP entry and pricing, immediate genuine proof (Google reviews link until verified excerpts available), authentic product comparison with direct contextual entry, optional help choosing a size, how delivery/loading/pickup works, local property-care and job proof, optional dated comparison, concise owner story, FAQs and final booking CTA. Keep one shopping hub, not forced research detours; retain distinct SEO pages.
- City: preserve 7 approved route slugs/templates and local evidence; city or ZIP may refine an estimate but an exact jobsite ZIP is needed to resolve a split zone. Do not fabricate jobs or availability.
- Size: use canonical three-day price, included weight, verified dimensions and approximate capacity, practical project examples, optional weight guidance, adjacent size guidance with actual value comparison, first-party proof and context-preserving size CTA.
- Pricing: clearly distinguish rental base, delivery fee, additional days, included weight vs billable excess and material-specific charges (only when verified/supported), required taxes/fees and missing data. Never substitute legacy daily_overage for prepaid Days Plus. Offer estimate before collecting contact information.
- Comparison: optional consideration-stage tool, dated evidence and original source, comparable terms/conditions, honest cheaper alternatives and missing data. Do not interrupt checkout or distort rankings.
- Service areas: honest zone estimates, address resolution and unknown/out-of-area assistance. Materials/FAQ: answer first, then help, with common restrictions and safety rules unchanged. About: human local ownership and proof; Blog: useful authentic content and contextual next action. Legal pages: do not rewrite approved legal/consent language as part of a conversion copy pass.
- A direct rental action remains possible without the comparison or project helper. Preserve existing URL, metadata, indexability, canonicals, schema, bilingual parity and navigation unless separately approved. No decorative animation within checkout; reduce motion and respect keyboard access. Use approved brand tokens, accessible contrast, visible labels/errors, real images and plain copy (target 6th–7th grade; established readability check applies).
- Help and failure paths: if pricing cannot load, say so; if location unresolved, ask for exact service ZIP; if no dates, offer alternative dates/sizes only when actually available and a consent-based inquiry option only when canonical intake is enabled.

## Checkout requirements

Retain the locked functional sequence from archive §20.7: choose size → schedule → one Unified Customer Details entry → Review & Pay with embedded Stripe → verified confirmation. A three-stage progress indicator (Size, Schedule, Checkout) can group the two checkout screens; it does not remove review/pay or force re-entry. Use contextual extras rather than a mandatory upsell screen, subject to owner visual sign-off. Additional days belong with schedule; size upgrade with size; prepaid weight and accepted special items with project/material decisions; only offer supported, server-priced options. An accepted upgrade revalidates capacity, restrictions, date/hold and price; decline is easy and neutral. Preserve previously reviewed early delivery as a future capacity-limited upgrade, not a promise. Do not offer weight above the equipment-specific safe payload, or treat purchased disposal allowance as permission to overload. Prepaid weight estimates are ranges, and unused prepaid weight acknowledgement remains as specified in archive §9.1.

- Desktop: sticky itemized rental summary and explicit Edit links. Mobile: sticky expandable summary plus next action within reach, no overlap with Randy/consent/footer, safe-area padding, keyboard visibility, price changes announced without distracting motion. Preserve existing locked summary behavior from booking rev 2.
- Schedule: duration-first or date-first editing, full interval and delivery/pickup rule verification, 3–30 days under Days Plus and 31+ human quote. Date, size, duration, service ZIP and add-on edits invalidate dependent quote/availability/hold and require revalidation. No invented extras, date rules, fake slots or pricing.
- Unified Details: Home, Business and Contractor lanes; conditional business/company and separate billing/jobsite address fields; service state GA; actual service ZIP governs delivery fee, not billing ZIP. Prefill only authorized data from CSR/rebook holds; explain contact use; collect service and marketing consent separately under the existing approved policy, never require marketing. No real card data in a prototype.
- Review & Pay: itemized server-authoritative amount including fees/tax/discount, quote expiry and inventory verification, terms/cancellation shown before a single Pay $[total] action, dynamically eligible Stripe methods only, editable selections without retyping. No fake trust seals, wallet buttons or charges. Do not claim booking or text was sent until confirmed by authoritative state.
- Confirmation: verified booking number, receipt, what happens next (prepare site, delivery, load safely, pickup), contact/help and authorized rental management. No post-payment upsell before reassurance; optional attribution survey only per archive §20.7. Retain existing finalizer/webhook and CRM.

## Conversion intelligence and testing

Measure acquisition → paid booking using existing frozen events and new approved versioned events where required. Distinguish no inventory, lookup failure, missing rate, price objection, future research, payment failure, consent-based assisted sales, genuine abandonment and unknown outcomes; never label a competitor click as a lost sale. Optional save/resume may use consent-appropriate non-PII context or an expressly requested secure resume link; no automatic marketing or contact creation. Use canonical intake with anti-bot validation for requested callbacks. No personal details in analytics, mask sensitive fields, honor cookie and SMS categories. Track qualified visitors, size/schedule/checkout completion, availability rejections, calls/texts, paid bookings, average ticket, contribution margin and revenue per visitor, with denominators, channel/device segments and operational capacity noted. Use the November 22 funnel analysis as historical baseline, not current traffic or a conversion guarantee. Start with representative customer task tests; only run statistically credible A/B tests when traffic supports them. Record hypothesis, baseline, metric, guardrail, test result and decision. No percentage lift assumed.

- Little Junkers: [November 22 booking-flow plan](phase-2/BOOKING-FLOW-PLAN-2026-11-22.md), [copy voice audit](phase-2/COPY-VOICE-AUDIT-2026-11-22.md), existing design review Rev 2/PR #14 Rev 3, and November 28 owner-reviewed recommendations. ## Change control

Every CX PR must link this standard and an append-only CHANGELOG entry: before/after, rationale/evidence, affected paths/route families, canonical data boundary, test result vs not run, open decision and rollback/approval gate. Do not overwrite historical approval records. Keep existing checks and add focused regression checks when changing an experience contract. Changes to locked architecture, operations, price, privacy/consent, legal claims, SEO routes or production require explicit owner approval. No merge or go-live by assumption.
